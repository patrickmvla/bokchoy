## What this means for the wedge

The original framing — "Africa-optimized PlayFab" or "low-ARPU mobile F2P economy platform" — is dead. Three structural reasons:

1. **TAM is too small.** 85% of studios <$100K/year ARR cannot fund B2B SaaS contracts at any reasonable price.
2. **Wrong product for the dominant monetization.** Ads-monetized games don't need currency ledgers, gacha math, or sink/faucet models. The whole "economy engine" category is mismatched to the dominant African revenue model.
3. **Wrong constraint.** Retention is the binding constraint. A tool that solves retention is a different product (analytics + cohort + UA + hybrid monetization) from an economy engine.

## What might be a wedge if reframed

These are *candidates*, not validated wedges. None of them are "game economy engine" as originally scoped:

- **Retention infrastructure for emerging-market mobile-first studios.** Cohort analysis, push, UA attribution, hybrid ads/IAP optimization. Binding-constraint product.
- **Mobile-money payment rails for global games entering Africa.** Pay1st as a B2B category — but customer is global studios entering Africa, not African studios. Different ICP.
- **Services + publishing model.** Carry1st's actual shape. Not SaaS. Different business model, longer to scale, different funding profile.

## Decision impact

The "Africa as primary slice" line is closed. The user owes a decision:
- Pivot to one of the reframed wedges above (different category from the landscape survey)
- OR drop Africa as primary scope and pick a slice from the global landscape (mobile F2P / cosmetic shop / sports card / MMO).
